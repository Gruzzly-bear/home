Native Planting Guides & The Official "Kill It With Fire" Watchlist

⚠️ The Hit List (Eradicate on Sight)

Mother Nature makes mistakes, and suburban garden centers sell them for $19.99. If you see these freeloaders, rip them out, salt the earth, or poison the stumps. No mercy.

  • Bradford Pear: Smells like rotting fish and hot garbage. Branches shatter if a butterfly sneezes on them. Replace with a Dogwood before it crushes the fence.
  • Japanese Barberry: The "Tick-Breeder 9000." Creates a humid dome of misery where deer ticks spawn. Dig it out.
  • Norway Maple: Casts a shadow so dark it literally suffocates everything beneath it. The ultimate shade troll.
  • English Ivy: Looks "quaint" until it pulls a 50-foot oak tree down onto the roof. Cut it at the base and let it rot.

  • Tree of Heaven (Ailanthus altissima): The mothership for Spotted Lanternflies. Smells like rancid peanut butter. If you chop it, it spawns 50 angry clones. You MUST use herbicide on the freshly cut stump.
  • Autumn Olive: A shrub that alters soil nitrogen to favor itself and forms impenetrable walls of spite.
  • Multiflora Rose: An aggressive thorny shrub that overtakes pastures. Arching stems root wherever they touch the ground. Total nightmare fuel.

  • Japanese Knotweed: Will literally eat your house's foundation. Roots break through concrete. Nuke it from orbit.
  • Oriental Bittersweet: Wraps around tree trunks like a boa constrictor and strangles them to death.
  • Garlic Mustard: Engages in chemical warfare by poisoning the soil so native plants can't grow. Smells like garlic when crushed.
  • Bindweed (Wild Morning Glory): The zombie vine. Roots go 20 feet deep. Pulling it only makes it angry.
  • Purple Loosestrife: Pretty purple flowers that will absolutely annihilate local wetlands.
  • Poison Hemlock: Looks like giant Queen Anne's Lace with purple splotches on the stem. Drink this and die like Socrates. Seriously, wear gloves and DO NOT BURN IT.

  • Spotted Lanternfly: Red, polka-dotted, and drinks tree sap until the tree dies. Stomp on sight. Scrape their mud-like egg masses off trees in winter.
  • Emerald Ash Borer: A shiny green beetle that has murdered basically every Ash tree in PA. If we have Ash trees left, they need treatment ASAP.
  • Brown Marmorated Stink Bug: Smells like cilantro gone wrong. Wants to winter inside our house. Vacuum them up and flush them.

🌳 The "We Won't Starve" Native Roster

The stuff we actually *want* growing in the yard. Plant these in the fall so we can eventually abandon the grocery store entirely.

Layer Plant The Payoff
Canopy (Big Trees) American Persimmon Sweet fruit. Do not eat until wrinkly unless you hate your mouth.
Canopy (Big Trees) Shagbark Hickory Delicious nuts. Bark looks cool and houses bats (which eat the bugs we hate).
Understory (Small Trees) Pawpaw Banana-mango custard fruit. Plant at least 2 or they refuse to fruit.
Understory (Small Trees) Red Mulberry Massive berry yields. Get the native RED one, not the trash Asian White one.
Shrubs Elderberry & Hazelnut Hazelnuts for snacking, Elderberries for medicinal syrups so we don't get sick.
Groundcover & Roots Sunchokes (Jerusalem Artichoke) Underground potato-things. Very aggressive, will take over its corner.
Perennials Asparagus & Rhubarb Plant once, eat for 20 years. The ultimate lazy-man's crop.
