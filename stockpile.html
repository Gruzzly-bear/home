💰 $150/Month Trickle-Stockpile Schedule

Month 1: Fats & Bulk Carbs

Spend $150 on 3 giant jugs of oil and 100 lbs of white rice. Store the rice in sealed buckets to protect against basement moisture.

Month 2: Dense Proteins

Spend $150 entirely on flats of canned chunk chicken, tuna, and Spam. Hunt for deals at BB's Grocery Outlet.

Month 3: Hydration & Oats

Buy two stackable 5-gallon water bricks, a gravity water filter (Sawyer/Berkey), and a 50 lb bag of rolled oats.

Month 4 & Beyond: Expand & Rotate

Focus on canned fruits in juice, gummy multivitamins, and medical supplies. Rotate older canned goods into regular monthly meals.
